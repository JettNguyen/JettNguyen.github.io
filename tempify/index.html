Five ways to be wrong about music

Each game is its own format with its own colour. Play one, play all five, come back tomorrow for a new set.

  1. 01

    One Bar

    Name the song from a half-second clip in up to six attempts. Every wrong guess unlocks a little more audio.

  2. 02

    Hit or Miss

    Did the track crack the Hot 100, or did it disappear without ever charting? Pick a side.

  3. 03

    Sampled

    Hear the flip, then find the source. Four candidates, one of them is the record it was built on.

  4. 04

    Era

    No hints. No context. No artist. Just the music, and the decade it came out of.

  5. 05

    Cover or Not

    Is this the original, or is someone else's song wearing it? Harder than it sounds.

  6. ↻

    Tomorrow

    A fresh set drops at midnight EST. Miss a day and the puzzle goes to the archive.
